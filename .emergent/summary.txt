**key DB schema**
- The application uses MongoDB, so there are no rigid table schemas. However, the data is structured based on the initial request, with collections for , , , , , and .
  - **users**: stores user profile data including name, role, location (city, country), bio, skills, interests, etc.
  - **swipes**: records user actions (interested/skip) on other profiles.
  - **matches**: stores pairs of users who have mutually liked each other.
  - **messages**: stores chat messages between matched users.
